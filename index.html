Data Import / Export

Backup, transfer and restore AKHADA data using Excel or CSV files.

Export Data

Download your selected data as Excel or CSV.

Import Data

Import members or other supported records from Excel/CSV. Existing records will not be silently deleted.

Backup & Safety

  • Export can be used as a regular backup.
  • Import will validate the selected data type before processing.
  • Financial records remain subject to Owner-only edit/delete permissions.
  • Import/restore actions should be restricted to Owner in the production version.

Trainer / Member dashboard privacy

Pending Dues and Today's Collection are Owner/Manager financial information. They are hidden from Trainer and Member accounts.

Gym Branding

Change AKHADA name, tagline and logo.

Data isolation

Member accounts are scoped to their own member ID. A member cannot browse the member directory or access another member's attendance, payments, workout, diet or progress. Trainer access is limited to assigned members. These restrictions must also be enforced by the production backend/database, not only by hiding menu items.
